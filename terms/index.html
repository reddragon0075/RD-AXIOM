The rules for using AXIOM FLEET.

These terms describe the relationship between AXIOM FLEET and organisations using the platform.

Last updated · 19 September 2026
On this page1. Using the platform2. Accounts3. Data & content4. Payments5. Liability
This page is product copy for the AXIOM FLEET prototype and is not legal advice. Before launch, this document should be reviewed and approved for the final contracting entity, plan terms and applicable Indian law.

1. Using the platform

AXIOM FLEET provides software for car-rental operations, corporate travel administration, driver workflows, reporting and related communications. By accessing the service, the organisation agrees to use it only for lawful business operations and in accordance with its subscription or order form.

Acceptable use

You must not use the platform to violate law, infringe another person’s rights, interfere with the service, probe security controls, upload malicious code or attempt to access another tenant’s information.

2. Accounts and access

The customer is responsible for maintaining accurate organisation information, protecting credentials and assigning access only to authorised users. The customer must promptly disable a user who leaves the organisation or no longer needs access. AXIOM FLEET may require additional authentication for sensitive actions.

3. Data and content

The customer retains its rights in operational data submitted to the service. The customer authorises AXIOM FLEET to process that data to provide, secure, support and improve the service. Each organisation is responsible for having the notices, consents and permissions required to collect driver, passenger, employee and supplier data.

4. Fees, trials and payments

Fees, billing periods, usage measures, taxes and renewal terms are set out in the applicable order form or plan. Unless stated otherwise, fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Payment gateway, mapping, messaging and telephony providers may have separate charges or availability terms.

5. Warranties, suspension and liability

The service is provided with commercially reasonable care. No software can promise uninterrupted operation, and third-party integrations may experience outages. AXIOM FLEET may suspend access for security, legal or non-payment reasons, with reasonable notice where practical. Liability limitations, indemnities and governing law should be finalised in the signed customer agreement.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to legal@axiomfleet.com.

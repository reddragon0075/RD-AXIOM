Important context for estimates, integrations, operational decisions and third-party services.

AXIOM FLEET is an operations platform. It supports decisions; it does not replace the customer’s operational judgement, statutory compliance review, professional advice or emergency services.

Product information

Website copy, screenshots, roadmaps and feature descriptions may describe planned, pilot or configurable capabilities. Availability depends on the customer plan, implementation, device, region, integration and release status. A feature is only committed when it is included in the applicable order form or written service description.

Calculations, estimates and reports

Billing previews, impact calculator outputs, route distances, tax estimates, utilisation metrics, emissions estimates and report values depend on the data and configuration supplied by the customer. They should be reviewed before being used for invoicing, statutory filing, payroll, safety decisions or financial forecasts.

Maps and third-party services

Maps, geocoding, messaging, payment, telephony, accounting, HRMS and other connected services are provided or controlled by third parties. AXIOM FLEET does not guarantee their accuracy, availability, delivery timing or continued compatibility. Their own terms, privacy policies and charges may apply.

Customer responsibility

Customers are responsible for lawful collection of personal data, document validity, driver and vehicle compliance, GST decisions, approvals, pricing, emergency response, user permissions and the final review of operational records. The SOS and communication features do not replace local emergency authorities or the customer’s safety procedures.

Simple terms for changing course.

How subscriptions, trials, implementation services and cancellations work for AXIOM FLEET customers.

This is a draft policy for the AXIOM FLEET prototype. Final commercial terms should be aligned with the customer order form, payment provider rules and applicable Indian consumer and tax requirements.

1. Trials and onboarding

Trial access, if offered, is provided for evaluation and may have limits on users, duties, storage, integrations or support. Trial data may be deleted after the stated evaluation period unless the customer converts or requests an export. Implementation, migration and custom configuration work may be separately scoped and billed.

2. Cancellation

A customer may request cancellation through its authorised administrator or written notice to the account team. The applicable notice period, renewal date and end-of-service date are governed by the order form. Unless agreed otherwise, cancellation does not remove amounts already due or committed minimums.

3. Refunds and credits

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable after the billing period begins, except where required by law or expressly agreed in writing. We may issue a prorated credit for a confirmed duplicate charge or a material service failure under the applicable service commitment. Third-party fees, taxes, implementation fees, usage charges and gateway charges may be non-refundable.

4. Service issues and failed payments

If a customer believes it has been charged incorrectly, it should contact support within 30 days with the invoice and transaction reference. We will investigate logs, payment records and the applicable plan. Failed or reversed payments may pause new usage, trigger retries or place the account into read-only mode after notice.

5. How to request help

Send cancellation, refund or billing questions to billing@axiomfleet.com. Include organisation name, account email, invoice number, transaction reference and the reason for the request. We will acknowledge the request and communicate the next step.

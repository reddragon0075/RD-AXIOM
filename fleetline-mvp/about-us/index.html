The product we are building

One operating loop, four useful surfaces.

AXIOM FLEET is designed for the people who coordinate a journey from request to close-out. Each surface is useful on its own; together they create a shared operational record.

For fleet and rental operators

Manage bookings, duties, driver readiness, vehicle records, supplier handoffs, pricing and proof from a tenant-scoped command centre. Empty data stays empty; the platform does not invent a roster or a financial result.

  • Dispatch and allotment with clear ownership
  • Offline-aware driver workflows for the field
  • Billing evidence that can be traced back to a duty

For corporate travel teams

Keep employee access, booking policies, approval decisions and supplier scope in one controlled workspace. An invite-only Network connects approved partners without turning the product into a public marketplace.

  • Branch and role-based visibility
  • Lead-time, cancellation and approval guardrails
  • Passenger close-out and audit-ready evidence

We are deliberate about the rollout: validate the Driver physical-device workflow first, learn from a controlled pilot, and only then expand Passenger experiences.

Prorating Employee Transport: The Mathematics of Per-Seat Shared Shuttle Cost Allocations

How corporate facility teams allocate shared employee shuttle costs across business units and cost centres using passenger seat-kilometre formulas.

Allocating employee shuttle expenses fairly across corporate business units and departmental cost centres is an accounting headache for enterprise facility teams. When a 20-seat corporate shuttle transports engineers, operations staff, and sales executives on a single morning route, billing the entire bus expense to a single departmental cost centre distorts financial reporting and triggers internal budget disputes.

Automating per-seat shared pool costing prorates route expenses based on exact passenger seat-kilometres.

1. The Shortcomings of Flat Departmental Splits

Enterprises typically attempt shared shuttle accounting through two flawed methods:

  • Equal Flat Division: Dividing the monthly vehicle charter cost equally among participating business units regardless of actual passenger headcounts.
  • Headcount Percentage Allocation: Allocating costs based on total department headcount rather than actual physical shuttle usage, penalizing remote teams.

Both methods fail to account for passenger pickup distances: an employee boarding five kilometres from office consumes far less fuel and vehicle capacity than an employee boarding thirty kilometres away.

2. The Passenger-Seat-Kilometre (PSKM) Allocation Model

The most equitable corporate transport allocation framework calculates cost using individual Passenger-Seat-Kilometres (PSKM):

$$PSKM_{employee} = Distance\,from\,Pickup\,to\,Campus\,(km)$$

$$Total\,Route\,PSKM = \sum_{i=1}^{n} PSKM_i$$

$$Department\,Cost\,Share = Route\,Base\,Expense \times \frac{\sum PSKM_{dept}}{Total\,Route\,PSKM}$$

Under this model:

  1. Every passenger pickup and drop coordinate records through the mobile passenger boarding pass.
  2. The platform calculates exact route distance for each employee's segment.
  3. Monthly corporate invoices automatically split shared shuttle bills into clean, auditable cost-centre debit lines for general ledger booking.

3. Dynamic Roster Adjustments in Axiom Mobility

Manual passenger-kilometre accounting is impossible on paper. Axiom Mobility automates the entire calculation loop:

  • Real-Time Boarding Verification: Passenger boarding OTPs and QR scans record exact boarding timestamps, ensuring unboarded employees do not bill to departmental ledgers.
  • Automated Cost-Centre Mapping: Every employee profile links to their SAP / Oracle ERP cost-centre code.
  • Integer Paise Financial Math: All proration math calculates in integer paise to eliminate fractional rounding discrepancies between departmental ledgers.

4. Financial Clarity for Enterprise Controllers

Implementing passenger-seat proration delivers measurable accounting rigor:

  • Departmental heads receive transparent monthly transport expense breakdowns backed by actual employee ride logs.
  • Facility directors optimize vehicle sizing by identifying under-utilized shuttle routes with low passenger-kilometre density.
  • Corporate finance teams post pre-reconciled cost-centre journal entries directly into enterprise ERP systems without manual spreadsheet formulas.

Exporting B2B Car Rental Sales Vouchers to Tally Prime: XML Schemas & Multi-Ledger Split

How automated Tally Prime XML export bridges operational fleet trip sheets with enterprise double-entry general ledgers and GST allocations.

Finance teams in commercial fleet agencies spend dozens of hours every month manually keying trip sheets and paper duty slips into accounting software. When an enterprise operates hundreds of daily corporate commutes across local hourly rentals, outstation trips, and airport transfers, manual entry creates ledger reconciliation delays and GST filing bottlenecks.

A direct XML bridge to Tally Prime translates operational duty data into canonical accounting entries in seconds.

1. The Anatomy of a Commercial Fleet Sales Voucher

A single commercial car rental transaction involves multiple tax and revenue ledgers:

  1. Party Ledger (Debtor): The corporate client account debited with the total billable amount.
  2. Service Revenue Ledger: The base revenue credited under SAC 996601 passenger transport.
  3. Extra Mileage & Waiting Revenue: Split line items for billable overtime hours and excess kilometres beyond base package slabs.
  4. Tax Allocations: Output CGST and SGST ledgers (or IGST for interstate outstation trips).
  5. Pass-Through Reimbursements: Fastag toll fees and airport parking charges billed at cost.

2. Generating Valid Tally XML Envelopes

Tally Prime requires a strict <ENVELOPE> XML hierarchy with exact case-sensitive tags and static variables.

A canonical sales voucher generated by Axiom Mobility formats as follows:

<TALLYMESSAGE xmlns:UDF="TallyUDF">
  <VOUCHER VCHTYPE="Sales" ACTION="Create">
    <DATE>20261116</DATE>
    <VOUCHERTYPENAME>Sales</VOUCHERTYPENAME>
    <VOUCHERNUMBER>INV-BK-202611-001</VOUCHERNUMBER>
    <REFERENCE>BK-202611-001</REFERENCE>
    <PARTYLEDGERNAME>Infosys Corporate Travel Desk</PARTYLEDGERNAME>
    <NARRATION>Corporate Car Rental Duty BK-202611-001</NARRATION>
    <ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
      <LEDGERNAME>Infosys Corporate Travel Desk</LEDGERNAME>
      <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>Yes</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>
      <AMOUNT>-3150.00</AMOUNT>
    </ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
    <ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
      <LEDGERNAME>Car Rental Service Revenue</LEDGERNAME>
      <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>
      <AMOUNT>3000.00</AMOUNT>
    </ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
    <ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
      <LEDGERNAME>Output CGST @ 2.5%</LEDGERNAME>
      <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>
      <AMOUNT>75.00</AMOUNT>
    </ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
    <ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
      <LEDGERNAME>Output SGST @ 2.5%</LEDGERNAME>
      <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>
      <AMOUNT>75.00</AMOUNT>
    </ALLLEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
  </VOUCHER>
</TALLYMESSAGE>

3. Resolving Party Ledger Mappings

A frequent defect during ERP imports is ledger name mismatch between operational customer tags and general ledger charts of accounts.

Axiom Mobility handles this through an integrated mapping table:

  • Every corporate client record links to a canonical tally_ledger_name attribute.
  • If an operational customer record lacks a direct match, the export engine flags the transaction before XML generation rather than producing corrupt import logs.
  • Special characters in passenger names and addresses escape properly (&amp;, &lt;, &gt;) to preserve XML validity.

4. Batch Import Workflow

Exporting monthly sales data no longer requires technical database exports:

  • Step A: Select the target billing period and approved customer proforma invoices.
  • Step B: Choose the "Export Tally XML" action from the billing ledger view.
  • Step C: Open Tally Prime, navigate to Import Data > Vouchers, and select the downloaded XML file.
  • Step D: Review imported vouchers with party ledgers, tax allocations, and bill-wise references reconciled automatically.

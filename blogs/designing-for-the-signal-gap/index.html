Designing for the Signal Gap: 4-Hour Offline Resilience in Field Transport

Offline-first is not a checkbox. It is an operational promise that drivers in basement parking ramps and highway dead zones can continue executing trips with local cryptographic queues.

The Reality of Basement Pickups

Enterprise car rental pickups frequently happen in multi-level underground parking ramps or airport basement terminals where cellular connectivity drops completely. Standard web apps lock or display connection error screens, forcing drivers to revert to paper slips or guess OTPs.

The Local Cryptographic Queue

Axiom Mobility's driver ecosystem operates on an offline-first contract: the mobile PWA runs a local IndexedDB transactional queue capable of storing up to four hours of full operational telemetry. OTP verification, passenger boarding stamps, and odometer entries execute deterministically on-device with SHA-256 local signing.

When the vehicle emerges onto the road and cellular signal restores, the background sync queue replays events in exact chronological order without supervisor intervention.

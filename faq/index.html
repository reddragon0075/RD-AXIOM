KNOWLEDGE BASE & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Axiom Mobility: statutory safety compliance, dual-ledger accounting, anti-fraud telemetry, and dynamic dispatch.

What is statutory night-shift women's safety compliance in corporate employee transport (ETMS)? +
Statutory night-shift compliance requires enterprise transport systems to enforce mandatory security escort assignments between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM whenever a female employee is the first pickup or last drop on a route. In Axiom Mobility, dispatch is hard-locked: vehicle trips cannot start or close without verified security guard credentials and multi-tier safe-reach confirmations (Push notification, automated IVR call, and P0 safety command escalation).
How does dual-ledger accounting work in B2B corporate car rental platforms? +
Dual-ledger accounting simultaneously evaluates Client Gross Receivable (what the corporate client owes) and Supplier Net Payable (what the fleet vendor or driver earns) on every trip duty. It calculates tax splits, GST SAC 996601 Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) rules, and applies an automatic Negative Margin Audit Hold whenever supplier cost exceeds client receivable, stopping margin leaks before invoices close.
What is the 3-way distance anti-fraud audit in fleet management? +
The 3-way distance anti-fraud audit cross-references physical OBD-II / CAN-bus vehicle telemetry, mobile driver app GPS trails, and Fastag toll plaza timestamp checkpoints. It identifies odometer tampering, unauthorized route detours exceeding 1.5 km, and synthetic GPS spoofing in real time.
How does automated multi-vendor SLA tier demotion work? +
Axiom Fleet tracks vendor response latencies, driver cancellations, and on-time arrivals against contractual SLAs. When an associate fleet vendor breaches threshold limits, the platform automatically demotes their allocation tier for future bookings and issues automated penalty debit notes without requiring manual supervisor intervention.
What billing models and tariff structures does Axiom Fleet support? +
Axiom Fleet supports 6 enterprise tariff engines: Local Spot Hourly/Kilometre Slabs (4H/40K, 8H/80K, 12H/120K), Outstation Calendar-Day with driver allowances, Radial Zone Rings for airport transfers, Per-Seat Shared Employee Shuttle Pooling, Monthly Fixed-plus-Variable dry/wet leases, and Fuel-Indexed dynamic rate escalations.

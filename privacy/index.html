Legal · Privacy Policy

Your data deserves visible care.

How AXIOM FLEET collects, uses, protects and retains information across its website and platform.

Last updated · 19 September 2026
On this page1. Information we collect2. How we use it3. Sharing4. Retention5. Your rights
This privacy page is a prototype policy intended for review against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any rules, sector guidance or contractual obligations applicable at launch.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account and contact details, organisation and billing information, support communications, device and log data, booking and duty records, driver and vehicle information, passenger details, location points, proof files and consent or preference records. We collect only what is needed for a stated purpose.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate duties, calculate charges, deliver alerts, provide route and tracking views, generate invoices, process support, prevent misuse, monitor reliability and meet legal or contractual obligations. We may use aggregated or de-identified information to understand service performance.

Consent and purpose

Where consent is the appropriate basis, we record the purpose, policy version, time and channel. Organisations using AXIOM FLEET remain responsible for communicating their own employee, passenger and driver notices.

3. Sharing and processors

We may share data with authorised subprocessors for hosting, storage, mapping, messaging, payments, telephony, analytics, security and support. Provider access is scoped, logged and governed by contractual controls. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention and security

Retention is based on purpose, customer instruction, legal requirements and the platform retention matrix. Financial records and audit trails may need longer retention. We use encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, access controls, audit logs, backups and incident response procedures.

5. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law and legitimate exceptions, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or information about processing. Requests can be routed through the customer organisation or sent to privacy@axiomfleet.com. We may need to verify identity and preserve information under a lawful hold.

Children and updates

The service is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the service, law or processing changes and will publish the revised date.
